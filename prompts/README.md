# Prompt library

Every prompt here produced a video Katechon published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. No made-up examples: each file has the output links, the exact prompts (frame, video and song), what made it work, and a one-tap Cast link that aims at the same video.

## Phone-POV livestream

The character films herself on her own phone: one continuous take, eye level, talking straight into the lens.

- [Malfoid reads her hate mail live](hate-mail.md) (30 s): A storytime livestream at breakfast: she reads insults, burns one, gets screamed at by an enchanted red letter, and melts at the last, kind note.

## Dance to a song

A choreographed dance locked to a song that goes into the model as an audio reference.

- [Malfoid dances on the tower at blue hour](look-up.md) (30 s): A solo 30-second dance livestream: Malfoid dances to an original song on a castle tower at dusk, then a gust of wind wrecks her hair and she insists it was intentional.
- [Malfoid and two friends dance on the feast table](pure-gold.md) (30 s): A three-girl choreographed dance trend on a feast table in an empty candlelit hall, one dancer out of sync, and a creaking door that turns them into angels with books.

## Trend format

A known TikTok trend structure (walk-up, break-away, regroup) performed by your characters.

- [Malfoid, Thea and Odette walk in like they own it](own-it.md) (30 s): A walk-up-and-break-it-down trend: three girls strut toward a low camera in formation, break into solo moves on the beat switch, and snap back together.

## Tutorial / how-to

A borrowed creator format: ingredient, step, close-up, reveal, with a twist at the end.

- [Malfoid's love potion tutorial](love-potion.md) (30 s): A beauty-guru-style tutorial: rose petals, three stirs clockwise, a sniff of the steam, and a smell that gives away exactly who she likes.

## Explainer

Many short clips of one character, cut to a voice-over that teaches something.

- [Penny explains how to build a bank from scratch](penny-bank.md) (82 s): An 82-second money explainer: Penny, a low-poly banker, goes from gold under the mattress to deposits, paper money and interest.

## The pattern behind all of them

- **Paint the first frame, then animate it.** An image prompt fixes the face, outfit, set and light; the video prompt only has to describe motion and sound.
- **Make the camera a thing in the room.** "The phone propped on the table, eye level, the only viewpoint, no cuts" is what produces a believable one-take livestream.
- **Timestamp every beat.** 0-4s, 4-9s, 9-14s… with exact quoted dialogue. Seedance follows a timeline far better than a paragraph.
- **End on a turn.** A gag, a reveal or a change of heart in the last five seconds.

The longer write-up, including what gets blocked and what it costs, is in the [field guide](../guides/seedance-prompting-field-guide.md).
